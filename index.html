Who we are

A family for children who need one.

Du Merci Children Development Ministry, also known as DU Merci Orphanage, is a family-style children's home where every child is known by name, supported through school and prepared for a confident, independent future.

Our mission

To give every child in our care a safe home, quality education, good health and the love of a family.

Our vision

A community where no child grows up alone, unloved or without opportunity.

What we do

Complete care, from the first day to adulthood.

We care for the whole child — body, mind and heart — and stay beside them as they grow.

Safe shelter

Clean, family-style rooms with trained house parents on site day and night.

Education

School fees, uniforms, books, after-school tutoring and support into university or trade school.

Healthcare

Regular check-ups, vaccinations, health insurance and a partner clinic for emergencies.

Nutrition

Three balanced meals and snacks every day, planned with a nutritionist.

Counselling

Trauma-informed care and one-to-one counselling with a licensed therapist.

Life skills

Cooking, budgeting, digital skills, mentoring and vocational training for older children.

Play & creativity

Sports, music, art, a library and a playground — because childhood should be fun.

Aftercare

Help with housing, jobs and further study for young adults leaving our care.
